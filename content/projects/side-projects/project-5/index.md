## Algorithmic Investment Platform & ArXiv Research Integration

Production-grade personal investment system combining sophisticated data engineering, machine learning research, and distributed computing infrastructure. Active integration of latest ArXiv findings into quantitative strategy development.

### Infrastructure (Homelab)
- **Compute Cluster**: 22 CPU cores, 176GB RAM, 40GB VRAM, 5TB SSD storage
- **Distributed Processing**: Dask cluster for parallelized backtests, correlation analysis, parameter sweeps
- **Data Persistence**: PostgreSQL data lake with multi-terabyte historical market data
- **GPU Acceleration**: 40GB VRAM for deep learning (time series forecasting, anomaly detection, feature learning)

### Data Pipeline
- **Multi-Source Ingestion**: Price data, XBRL fundamentals, alternative datasets (sentiment, volume profiles)
- **ETL Infrastructure**: Automated pipelines with error recovery, data validation, schema versioning
- **Feature Engineering**: Advanced signal generation (technical indicators, regime detection, microstructure)
- **Checkpoint & Versioning**: Reproducible research with parameter tracking and experiment versioning

### Research Integration
- **ArXiv Monitoring**: Systematic tracking of ML/finance literature (reinforcement learning, time series, anomaly detection)
- **Paper → Strategy Pipeline**: Workflow from research discovery through backtesting to paper-validated implementation
- **Quantitative Methods**: Factor models, Bayesian inference, deep RL for portfolio optimization
- **SOTA Integration**: Latest techniques from academic literature tested on live market data

### ML Components
- **Time Series Models**: LSTM/Transformer architectures for price prediction and regime forecasting
- **Anomaly Detection**: Unsupervised learning for market microstructure changes
- **Portfolio Optimization**: Constrained optimization with transaction costs and regime-dependent risk
- **Reinforcement Learning**: DQN and PPO for adaptive trading policies

### Impact
Demonstrates sophisticated data engineering at scale. Shows ability to bridge academic research (ArXiv papers) with production systems. Hands-on experience with distributed computing, machine learning infrastructure, and quantitative finance. Active research integration with real capital at stake.
