Self-Hosted Research Tracker

Running a personal Redmine instance to manage the gap between reading research and doing something with it. SOTA moves fast; without a structured backlog, implementation ideas get lost.

What It Tracks

  • Paper implementation queue — ArXiv findings flagged for reproduction or adaptation
  • Open experiments — hypotheses from active projects that need controlled testing
  • Cross-project dependencies — work in the SPO, retrieval, and agentic projects that feeds into each other
  • Methodology gaps — techniques in use that need deeper validation or replacement

Why It Matters

Most engineers consume research. This is the infrastructure for doing something systematic with it. The Redmine instance is the operational layer behind the portfolio: the projects here started as tickets there.

Infrastructure

Self-hosted on homelab hardware. Previously exposed through a personal dynamic-DNS endpoint. Demonstrates comfort with self-managed infrastructure beyond cloud-only environments.
