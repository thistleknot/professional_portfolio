## Generative AI Studio: ComfyUI & Open-WebUI

Self-hosted generative AI platform demonstrating hands-on expertise with state-of-the-art creative and analytical AI tooling. Active experimentation with latest models and workflows.

### Core Infrastructure
- **ComfyUI Advanced Workflows**: Custom node chains for complex image generation, inpainting, LoRA composition, and multi-model orchestration
- **Open-WebUI Integration**: Unified interface to multiple LLM backends with conversation persistence and RAG capabilities
- **GPU Resource Management**: Optimized VRAM allocation (40GB) across concurrent generative and inference tasks
- **Real-time Execution**: Low-latency workflow execution with dynamic model loading and caching strategies

### Technical Focus
- **Diffusion Models**: Latest SD3, FLUX, and variant architectures
- **Multimodal Pipelines**: Vision-language model chains, image-to-text-to-image workflows
- **Model Composition**: LoRA stacking, ControlNet integration, attention mechanisms
- **Optimization**: Quantization, batch processing, memory-efficient inference

### Impact
Demonstrates active tracking of generative AI SOTA. Hands-on experience with production-scale GPU infrastructure. Direct involvement in emerging capabilities (FLUX, multimodal composition) as they reach maturity.
