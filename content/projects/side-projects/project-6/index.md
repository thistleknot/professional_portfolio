## Algorithmic Investment Platform & ArXiv Research Infrastructure

Comprehensive personal investment system combining quantitative research, machine learning, and market data infrastructure. Self-managed homelab providing research compute and data persistence for algorithmic trading strategy development.

### Infrastructure
- **Homelab Cluster**: 22 CPU cores, 176GB RAM, 40GB VRAM, 5TB SSD storage
- **Dask Distributed Computing**: Parallel analysis across market data, backtest simulations, and research workflows
- **PostgreSQL Data Lake**: Multi-TB historical market data (prices, fundamentals, alternative datasets)
- **GPU Acceleration**: 40GB VRAM for deep learning models (time series forecasting, anomaly detection)

### Features
- **Market Data Pipeline**: Automated ingestion of price data, XBRL fundamentals, and alternative data sources
- **Research Repository**: GitHub-managed strategy codebase with version control and experiment tracking
- **Quantitative Analysis**: Dask-accelerated backtesting, correlation analysis, portfolio optimization
- **ArXiv Integration**: Systematic tracking of relevant ML/finance papers for strategy inspiration
- **Strategy Development**: Rapid experimentation with rule-based and ML-based trading rules

### Technical Implementation
- **Data Engineering**: ETL pipelines for multi-source market data consolidation
- **Distributed Computing**: Dask for embarrassingly parallel tasks (backtests, parameter sweeps)
- **Machine Learning**: Time series forecasting, anomaly detection, portfolio optimization
- **Git-Tracked Workflows**: Reproducible experiment management with parameter versioning
- **Monitoring & Metrics**: Real-time portfolio tracking and strategy performance analytics

### Research Focus
- **ArXiv Paper Reviews**: Continuous monitoring of ML/finance literature for novel approaches
- **Quantitative Methods**: Factor models, regime detection, risk management
- **Deep Learning**: LSTM/Transformer models for sequence prediction and feature extraction
- **Alternative Data**: Sentiment analysis, volume analysis, market microstructure insights

### Impact
Demonstrates advanced data engineering, distributed computing, and quantitative finance expertise. Shows ability to build production-grade infrastructure for algorithmic research. Direct involvement in bridging academic research (ArXiv papers) with practical trading system implementation.
